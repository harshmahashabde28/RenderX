# Audio assets

All bundled audio files are pre-downloaded and ship with RenderX; no network access is required at runtime.

## Sound effects — Kenney

The UI click, confirmation, and locked-exit effects come from Kenney's [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) pack. Laser, impact, explosion, dash, and scan effects come from Kenney's [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) pack. The footsteps and pickup effects come from Kenney's [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) pack. These packs are released under Creative Commons CC0.

## Music — OpenGameArt

- `maze_theme.ogg`: “Project Utopia (seamless loop)” by congusbongus, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/project-utopia-seamless-loop), CC0.
- `shooter_theme.ogg`: “Chiptune Battle Music” by pmiller (loop version), [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-battle-music), CC0.

The authors and source pages are recorded here for project provenance; CC0 does not require attribution.
